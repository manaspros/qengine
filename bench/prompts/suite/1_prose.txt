Write a detailed, well-structured essay about the history of the printing press, from Gutenberg's movable type in fifteenth-century Mainz to the industrial rotary presses of the nineteenth century. Explain how the technology worked at each stage, who the key inventors and printers were, how printing changed the spread of religious, scientific and political ideas across Europe, and what economic forces drove its adoption. Use concrete examples and dates, and end with a reflection on what the printing press can teach us about how new information technologies change societies.
